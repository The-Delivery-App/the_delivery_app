Features
========

This is all the features that you are able to do on our delivery app.
 
Browsing the feed
-----------------
 
When you open the app, you get a scrollable feed of food items and restaurants near you. You can filter by tags (like "vegan" or "spicy"), price tier, and dietary preferences, and sort by rating, price, or popularity. There's also a full search screen if you know what you're looking for.
 
Restaurant pages
----------------
 
Tapping a restaurant takes you to its full menu. Each food item shows the name, price, rating, description, calorie count, and a thumbnail image.
 
Basket
------
 
Adding items builds up a basket. Before you check out you can review everything — quantities, subtotal, delivery fee, service fee, and any discount that applies.
 
Placing an order
----------------
 
When you're ready, you confirm your delivery address and place the order. The app generates an idempotency key behind the scenes to make sure the order can't be submitted twice by accident. You can also add delivery instructions if needed.
 
Splitting the bill
------------------
 
One of the more interesting features — orders can be split between multiple users. Each person in the group is tracked separately so everyone only pays their share.
 
Tracking your order
-------------------
 
Once the order is placed, you can follow it through its status updates (confirmed, preparing, out for delivery, etc.). There's also a map screen that shows your courier's live location as they make their way to you.
 
Accounts and sign-in
--------------------
 
Users sign in through Serverpod's identity provider integration. Your account stores saved delivery addresses, favourite items and restaurants, and your order history.
 
Reviews
-------
 
After receiving an order you can leave a review on any of the food items. These feed into each item's overall rating shown in the feed.
 
Special deals
-------------
 
Restaurants can run special deals that surface in the app as promoted or discounted items.
 
Settings
--------
 
A settings screen lets users manage their profile, saved addresses, and app preferences.
