Overview
========
 
The Delivery App is a full-stack mobile food delivery app made by Team 1b. The idea was to allow people to order food from nearby restaurants and have it delivered, while tracking the courier on a live map.
 
What the app lets you do
------------------------
 
- Browse a feed of restaurants and food items near you
- Search and filter by tags, price, and rating
- View a restaurant's full menu and individual dish details
- Add items to your basket and place an order
- Pay on your own or split the bill with friends
- Follow your order through each status update
- Watch your courier's location on a map as they head to you
- Save your favourite restaurants and dishes
- Leave reviews on food you've ordered
- Manage your account, addresses, and settings

How the repository is laid out
------------------------
 
The project is split into three packages:
 
- the_delivery_app_flutter — the Flutter mobile app that users actually interact with
- the_delivery_app_server — the backend server built with Serverpod
- the_delivery_app_client — a shared library auto-generated by Serverpod that lets the app talk to the server in a type-safe way
